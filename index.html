A quick introduction

Reading the signals microbes leave behind — and building tools that act on them.

I'm an aquaculture researcher and R&D team leader specialising in microbiome management. My work combines flow cytometry, 16S rRNA sequencing, bioinformatics, and machine learning to understand host–microbiome and microbe–microbe interactions.

I sit between academia and industry by choice — moving between fundamental biological questions, laboratory research, and tools people actually use in the field.
