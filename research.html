Research interests

Microbiome management for healthier and more resilient biological systems.

My research focuses on understanding microbial communities and translating their dynamics into practical, decision-ready tools. I combine flow cytometry, microbial community profiling, 16S rRNA sequencing, bioinformatics, and machine learning to study host–microbiome and microbe–microbe interactions.

Within aquaculture and related biological systems, I am particularly interested in microbial community fingerprinting, disease-risk indicators, production performance, predictive modelling, and the development of early-warning approaches.
